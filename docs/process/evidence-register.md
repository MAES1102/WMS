- `RECONSTRUCTED`: retrospectively inferred or supported only by intentionally excluded private material.
- `PLANNED`: future work not implemented.
- `UNVERIFIED`: historical claim lacks supporting durable evidence.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: attempted verification produced no result.
